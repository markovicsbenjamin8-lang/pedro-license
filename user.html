Features

Everything you need

Pedro Debug is built on top of Meteor Client, giving you a full suite of tools designed for serious players.

👁
Storage ESP
Highlights chests, shulkers, furnaces and more through walls with customizable colors and tracers.
🔴
Redstone ESP
Detects and renders repeaters, comparators, observers, TNT and redstone lamps across loaded chunks.
🗺
Sus Chunk Finder
Analyzes chunk data for unnatural plant growth and placed deepslate to locate hidden bases.
📷
Freecam
Detach your camera from your player and fly freely around the world to scout areas ahead.
🔧
Auto Tool
Automatically switches to the best tool in your hotbar for whatever block you're currently mining.
🗺
Region Map
Visualizes region file boundaries to help understand server world structure at a glance.
🔔
Spawner Notifier
Sends a chat alert and renders an ESP box whenever a spawner is detected in a loading chunk.
⚡
No Render Culling
Disables render culling so entities and blocks are rendered even when outside normal view range.

FAQ

Common questions

What Minecraft version does it support?›
Pedro Debug currently supports Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric. Make sure you have the Meteor Client mod installed as it depends on it.
How do I install it?›
Register an account, redeem your license key, then click Download. Place the downloaded .jar in your .minecraft/mods folder alongside Meteor Client and Fabric API.
Is it safe to use on servers?›
Pedro Debug uses only client-side rendering and does not send any modified packets to the server. It is undetected by all major server-side anti-cheat systems including NCP, AAC, Matrix and Grim.
Can I share my key?›
No. Keys are bound to your email and your HWID on first launch. If a mismatch is detected the client will not load. Sharing your key results in a permanent ban.
How do I get a key?›
Keys are distributed by the developer. Contact us to purchase access. Once you have a key, register an account here and redeem it from your dashboard.
